Title: Rotate until red bag is detected

Goal
- Rotate the robot to the right in discrete steps and stop when a red bag is visible in the camera frame.

Target stimulus
- A "red bag": a portable container (handbag, tote, backpack, grocery bag) with predominantly red material.
- Visual cues: red color area with bag-like shape (handles/straps, seams), possible logos, fabric sheen; ignore small red specks or flat red panels that are clearly not bags.

Procedure overview
- Capture an image each step.
- Promote the image into this episodic memory as frame_XX.jpg.
- Use automated analysis on each saved frame to check for the red bag.
- If detected, halt rotation and report the step and approximate bearing.
- If not detected after one full rotation, report not found and request next action.

Notes
- Use discrete right turns between captures (default step ~15 degrees) to avoid motion blur and enable quick stopping.
- Safety: keep stop command available; do not spin continuously.
- This guide may be appended with a per-frame log of detection results.
