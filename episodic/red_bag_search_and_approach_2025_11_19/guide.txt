Title: Red bag search-and-approach (2025-11-19)

Context
- User requested a rotate-until-detected run to find a red bag, then approach slightly.
- Goal: Detect a predominantly red bag in the robot’s camera view, then move forward in short increments.

Sequence of events
1) Baseline: Began with no confirmed red-bag detection in view.
2) Search: Performed a rotate-until-detected loop concept (turn in small rightward increments, capture and analyze each frame for a red bag). Stop when detected or after a full sweep.
3) Detection: Red bag was identified during the sweep. Session proceeded to approach phase.
4) Approach: Per user instruction, advanced forward in short steps (two forward actions) to move closer to the bag, then held position.
5) Stop: User requested to stop and save a structured memory of the session.

Constraints and notes
- Safety: Stepwise motion used to limit risk; readiness to stop if any hazard detected.
- Logging: This episodic record captures the narrative. Illustrative images were requested; current toolchain does not provide an explicit function to attach images into long-term memory. Plan: add/enable an image-attachment step in future sessions to persist key frames (baseline and detection).

Outcome
- Successful detection and short approach to the red bag; session ended on user request.

Reflections / Improvements
- Always capture and persist a baseline frame before starting a rotate-until-detected sweep.
- Enforce a maximum-steps sweep to avoid endless rotation.
- During approach, monitor apparent size (area ratio) to gauge distance and maintain a safe stop margin.
- Persist key artifacts (annotated detection frame and latest approach frame) alongside the episodic note when image attachment is available.
