Procedure: Turn toward visible gold and collect it with short perception-action steps.

1) Perceive
- Use capture_game_image.
- Use analyze_current_game_view with a specific question (e.g., “Where is the gold relative to my facing?”).

2) Align facing (short turns)
- If gold is right of centerline: turn_game_clockwise once, then re-check.
- If gold is left of centerline: turn_game_counterclockwise once, then re-check.
- Repeat single-step turns until gold is roughly centered (< ~6° offset).
- Save a snapshot after each turn.

3) Advance in short bursts
- Move forward one step with move_game_forward.
- Capture_game_image and analyze: if gold shrinks distance and remains ahead, continue; if it drifts left/right, go back to step 2.
- Repeat until “Gold collected” is returned or gold disappears from view (then re-scan by rotating slowly).

4) Save progress
- After every action (turn/move), save a snapshot (capture_game_image already does this) and keep latest_capture.jpg updated.

Notes:
- Avoid long action chains; alternate action and perception.
- If max iterations or errors occur, stop and report state.
