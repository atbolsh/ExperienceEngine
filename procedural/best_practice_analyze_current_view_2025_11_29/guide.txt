Title: Best practice for using analyze_current_view

When to use:
- You need immediate situational awareness from the robot camera during a multi-step task.

Key guidance:
1) Always provide a specific question. Examples:
   - "Do you still see the red bag in frame?"
   - "Is there anything blocking the path forward?"
   - "Which direction should I turn to center the doorway?"
2) Avoid running with default/no question. The default often returns long, unfocused paragraphs and may not answer what you actually care about.
3) Keep follow-ups iterative and targeted. Ask one focused question, act, then ask the next.
4) Re-run after each small motion to verify outcome (e.g., after a 1-second left turn, ask again: "Is the red bag still visible?").
5) Reference the attached image in your reasoning; explicitly state where objects are (e.g., "mid-right").

Mini-loop template:
- Step A: turn_robot_left (default 1s)
- Step B: analyze_current_view("Do you still see the red bag?")
- Step C: If yes, repeat A-B; if no, stop_robot_motion.

Notes:
- This minimizes verbose output and maintains context on the task at hand.
