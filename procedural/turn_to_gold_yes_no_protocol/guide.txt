Procedure: Turn-and-reach gold using yes/no alignment and single direction choice.

1) Query analyzer:
   Prompt: “we are playing as a green agent with a red eye. It moves in the direction of the red eye. Is the eye (relative to the green body) facing roughly toward the gold piece? Finish the answer with a 'yes' or a 'no'.”
2) If response is “yes”: move_forward 1–3 steps, then go to step 1.
3) If response is “no”: Ask ONCE per chain: “would it be faster to turn clockwise or counterclockwise? Answer 'clockwise' or 'counterclockwise'.” Record choice.
4) Turn exactly one step in the chosen direction (CW or CCW).
5) Return to step 1. Do not re-ask direction within the same chain.
6) Stop when analyzer reports “there is no gold piece” (assume gold collected) or if collisions/obstacles appear.
Notes:
- Keep the loop tight: analyze → single turn or short move → analyze.
- Avoid long action chains; reassess after every step.
- If walls are present, plan detours before proceeding forward.
